# Your home first

**Picture your own facade transformed, using the same method as the houses in this book.**

[IMAGE: demo_foto-real_antes + demo_foto-real_n2 — a real house photo and its AI "after"]

You need: a phone camera and an AI image tool that can **edit an uploaded photo**. Most general AI assistants with image features can do this. Check the tool's terms if you plan to share or sell the images.

---

## Step 1: Take the right photo

The AI can only keep your house the same if it can see it clearly.

1. **Stand straight in front of the house,** far enough back to fit the whole facade plus a little ground and sky. From the sidewalk or footpath is usually right.
2. **Hold the phone at chest height, level.** Not tilted up. Tilting makes walls lean and the AI may "fix" them by changing the house.
3. **Shoot on a bright overcast day, or with the sun behind you.** Avoid strong shadows across the front.
4. **Clear what you can:** bins, cars, hoses, toys. The AI may turn clutter into strange objects.
5. **Take a second photo at an angle** (about 45° from one corner). It helps you check the result.
6. **Remove private details before sharing:** house number, car plates, people.

**How you know it worked:** all windows, the door and both roof edges are fully in the frame and look straight.

---

## Step 2: Choose a level and colours

- Start with **Level 1** even if you want more. It shows how far a clean-up goes.
- Pick a palette from *Colour combinations*, or describe your own.

---

## Step 3: Use the master prompt

Upload your photo and paste this. Fill in the parts in [brackets].

```
Edit this photo of my house. Keep the house exactly the same: same camera angle
and framing, same roof shape, same number and position of windows and doors,
same porch, steps, walls, trees and neighbouring buildings. Do not add, remove
or move any structural element.

Change only:
[LIST OF CHANGES — copy one level below]

Keep as is: [ANYTHING YOU DON'T WANT TO CHANGE]

Realistic photo, soft natural daylight, outdoor lights off, no people, no text,
no logos, no house numbers.
```

### Level 1 changes (copy into the prompt)
```
- Remove weeds, overgrowth and clutter from the yard and walls.
- Lawn mowed and edged; clean, simple garden beds along the house with mulch
  and a few low shrubs.
- Path, steps and porch swept clean.
- Front door painted [COLOUR].
- Two potted plants by the door.
```

### Level 2 changes (upload your Level 1 image)
```
- Walls repainted [WALL COLOUR], trim, fascia and window frames repainted
  [TRIM COLOUR], smooth and fresh.
- Shutters (if any) painted [ACCENT COLOUR].
- Broken railings, fence boards or gutters repaired so they look complete.
- New simple mailbox and door hardware in [BLACK / BRONZE / BRASS].
```

### Level 3 changes (upload your Level 2 image)
```
- New roof in [COLOUR / MATERIAL], same roof shape.
- New concrete or paved path and steps, same position and number of steps.
- New outdoor wall lights beside the door, switched off.
```

**Always edit step by step:** make Level 1 from your photo, Level 2 from the Level 1 image, Level 3 from Level 2. Asking for everything at once from the original photo usually changes the house itself.

---

## Step 4: Check the result

Put the original and the new image side by side and tick each line.

- [ ] Same number of windows, in the same places
- [ ] Same door position and shape
- [ ] Same roof shape and chimney
- [ ] Same number of steps
- [ ] Trees, fence and neighbours unchanged (unless you asked)
- [ ] Only the changes you listed
- [ ] Nothing melted, floating or duplicated
- [ ] It looks like a photo, not a painting

### If something went wrong

| Problem | Fix prompt |
|---|---|
| A window or door moved or disappeared | "Restore the windows and door exactly as in the original photo. Change nothing else." (Upload the original too.) |
| The house got bigger or a new storey appeared | Start again from the previous level; ask for fewer changes at once. |
| Steps changed in number | "Make the steps the same number as in the original photo." |
| Lights glowing in daylight | "Turn the outdoor lights off." |
| Strange marks or objects | "Remove the [describe it] next to the [location]. Change nothing else." |
| Looks like a painting | Add: "photorealistic, natural colours, real materials." |

---

## Step 5: Turn the picture into a plan

Write down every change between your photo and the image you like. Then go to *From image to reality* and sort each one into **DIY**, **Check first** or **Pro**. That list is your plan, and the image is the reference you show a professional when you ask for quotes.

**Most common mistake:** falling for a Level 3 image and doing nothing. Start with the Level 1 list this weekend.
