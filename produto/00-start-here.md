## See your home transformed, and know what to do first

Most people look at a tired house and see one huge, expensive job. This book breaks it into steps you can picture, plan and start.

You will:
1. **See it first.** Use AI to picture your own facade before you spend anything.
2. **Pick a level.** Weekend, one month or full makeover.
3. **Know who does what.** Every change is marked DIY, Check first or Pro.
4. **Start in the right order.** So nothing gets redone.

## The three levels

Every example house is shown at three levels. You do not have to go all the way. Level 1 alone often changes how a house feels from the street.

| | Level 1: Weekend | Level 2: One month | Level 3: Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| What changes | Clean-up, garden, front door, small details | Paint on walls and trim, shutters, simple repairs | Roof, concrete, structure, outdoor wiring |
| Who usually does it | You | You, with care and some checks | Professionals |
| Effort | Low to Medium | Medium to High | Hire |

**Level 1 never includes** roof work, structural repairs or electrical work.

**Abandoned houses take longer.** When a yard is badly overgrown, the clear-up alone can fill a weekend. Houses 01 and 06 plan Level 1 over two weekends.

## Before you start

- **All images in this book are AI-generated illustrations.** They show what is possible, not a guaranteed result.
- **A picture is not a building plan.** Anything involving structure, roofs, wiring or gas needs a qualified professional.
- **Old houses can hide hazards.** Lead paint and asbestos are covered in *From image to reality*. Read that page before scraping, sanding or drilling outside.
- **Local rules may limit changes.** Homeowners associations, councils and heritage areas can control colours and materials.
- **No prices.** Costs vary too much by place and year. Effort is shown as Low, Medium or High. Shopping lists use generic products and typical quantities.
