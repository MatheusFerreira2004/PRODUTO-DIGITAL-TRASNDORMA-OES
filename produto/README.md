## Writing rules used
- US spelling; Canadian and Australian terms in the glossary.
- Imperial and metric units.
- No brands, no prices in currency, no earnings promises.
- Effort shown as Low / Medium / High.
- Every exterior job marked **DIY**, **Check first** or **Pro**.
- All images are AI-generated illustrations, stated on the first page.
