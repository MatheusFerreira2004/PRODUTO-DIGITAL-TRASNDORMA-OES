## The recipe

**The story:** a symmetrical two-storey house in faded beige. A warmer palette and a defined entry make it look established.

**Palette 04 · Ivory & Bronze**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#EEE8DA` | `#6B5A45` | `#6B5A45` | `#6B5A45` | Dark grey |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matching planters each side of the door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Reshape beds, low rounded evergreen shrubs | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door bronze-brown | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint shutters bronze (ground floor) | 2 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint walls and upper shutters | 2 | Pro (two storeys) | — |
| Bronze hardware, mailbox and numbers | 2 | DIY | Low |
| Small portico over the door | 3 | Pro (structure) | — |
| New brick path | 3 | Pro | — |

**Order:** garden layout → upper-floor painting (pro) → ground-floor details → planting last.

**Watch out:** two-storey walls and upper shutters need scaffolding or a lift. This is where Level 2 usually becomes a mix of DIY and pro.

**Most common mistake:** breaking the symmetry. On a colonial, plants, lights and pots should mirror each side of the door.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Clear-up kit (to remove the old shrubs)
- Door kit: deep bronze-brown exterior door paint
- Bed kit: an even number of low rounded evergreen shrubs, so each side matches
- Pot kit: two tall matching planters with clipped evergreens

### Level 1 plan: one weekend
| | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Remove old shrubs; mark the beds with string so both sides match. | Door: clean, sand, prime. |
| Sunday | Door: first coat. Plant the shrubs, mirroring each side. | Door: second coat. Mulch, planters, mow and edge. |

## Production

**Before:** two-storey symmetrical colonial house, side-gabled roof, centred front door with a small flat hood, five windows upstairs and four downstairs, faded beige vinyl siding, white trim, black shutters faded to grey, concrete path straight to the door. Condition: dated; overgrown uneven shrubs, worn lawn, plain flat door.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** shrubs replaced by neat rounded low evergreen shrubs in symmetrical beds; two tall matching black planters with topiary each side of the door; front door painted deep bronze-brown; lawn mowed and edged.
**Keep:** siding, shutters, roof, path, door hood.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** siding repainted warm ivory, all shutters painted bronze-brown, trim kept white-ivory; bronze door handle, mailbox and a plain plaque for numbers (no visible numbers).
**Keep:** roof, path, planters.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** small classical portico with two columns and a pediment over the front door; straight path rebuilt in red brick; new dark grey roof, same shape.
**Keep:** windows, door position, everything else.
