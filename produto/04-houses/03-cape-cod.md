## The recipe

**The story:** a classic storybook shape with peeling paint and weathered shingles. Strong contrast brings the charm back.

**Palette 09 · Navy Colonial**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#2F3E55` | `#FFFFFF` | `#B33A3A` | `#FFFFFF` | Charcoal |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean up beds, prune hedge, sweep path | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the door red | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Window boxes under the two front windows | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint walls navy and trim white (ground floor) | 2 | DIY, check first on old paint | High |
| Dormer trim | 2 | Pro (height) | — |
| Paint shutters white or remove | 2 | DIY | Medium |
| New roof | 3 | Pro | — |
| Rebuild front steps | 3 | Pro | — |

**Order:** clean-up → test paint → roof and dormer work (pro) → ground-floor paint → window boxes and garden.

**Watch out:** dormers are above roof level. Do not paint them from a ladder on the roof.

**Most common mistake:** dark walls with cream trim. On navy, trim needs a clean white to stand out.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Clear-up kit, plus hedge shears and a weeding tool for the path
- Door kit: classic red exterior door paint
- Bed kit: mulch for the front beds
- Two window boxes with brackets suited to the wall, potting mix, trailing plants

### Level 1 plan: one weekend
| | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Trim the hedge, weed beds and path. | Door: clean, sand (after a lead test on older homes), prime. |
| Sunday | Door: first coat. Mulch the beds. | Door: second coat. Fix and plant the window boxes. |

## Production

**Before:** small one-and-a-half-storey Cape Cod house, steep side-gabled roof with two gabled dormers, central front door, one double-hung window each side, grey weathered cedar shingle walls, white trim peeling, dark shutters faded, low hedge along the front, brick front step. Condition: worn; peeling trim, moss on the roof, patchy hedge, weeds in the path.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** hedge neatly trimmed, beds weeded and mulched, path swept and weeds removed; front door painted classic red; simple white window boxes with green trailing plants under the two front windows.
**Keep:** weathered shingles, peeling trim, faded shutters, mossy roof, dormers unchanged.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** walls painted deep navy, all trim and dormer fronts painted crisp white, shutters painted white; brass door knocker and handle.
**Keep:** roof, brick step, hedge, red door.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** new charcoal shingle roof, same shape and dormers; new brick front steps with a simple black handrail, same number of steps.
**Keep:** everything else.
