## The recipe

**The story:** a timber house on stumps with a deep verandah, left to the weather. Classic white and green brings back the character.

**Order:** clear → check stumps, stairs and materials → structural and roof work (pro) → paint → verandah styling and garden.

**Watch out:**
- **Under the house, look before you clear.** Old houses often have broken fibro sheets or offcuts stored or dumped underneath, and fibro made before 1990 may contain asbestos. Do not break, sweep dry, cut or bag these pieces yourself until they are tested. See *From image to reality*.
- Enclosed verandahs may also be lined with fibro. Older timber may have lead paint. Test before sanding.
- Heritage overlays can control colours: check with your council.

**Most common mistake:** painting a verandah whose stairs or stumps are moving. Fix the structure first.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Clear-up kit, plus a string trimmer and a disposable P2 mask for dusty clearing (this does not make asbestos safe to handle)
- Soft broom, bucket, mild cleaner for the verandah
- Bed kit: tropical shrubs suited to your area for two small beds by the stairs
- Four to six pots with ferns or other shade plants

### Level 1 plan: two weekends
| | Saturday | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| Weekend 1 | Walk around and under the house; note any fibro pieces and leave them alone. Cut and bag the yard overgrowth. | Clear the rest of the yard. Mow. Book an asbestos test if you found suspect pieces. |
| Weekend 2 | Clear under the house only where nothing suspect was found. Sweep and hand-wash the verandah. | Plant the beds by the stairs, mulch, set out the pots. |

## Production

**Before:** traditional Queenslander house, single storey raised on timber stumps about 6 ft (1.8 m) high, wide front verandah across the full front with white timber balustrade and decorative brackets, corrugated metal hip roof, central timber stairs to the verandah, timber lattice screening under the house, weatherboard walls. Condition: abandoned; paint peeling to grey timber, rusty roof, tall weeds and palms overgrown, broken lattice panels, sagging gutter.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** weeds and overgrowth cleared from the yard and from the visible area under the house; verandah swept clean; a few potted ferns on the verandah; simple garden bed either side of the stairs with tropical shrubs; lawn mowed.
**Keep:** peeling paint, rusty roof, broken lattice, gutter.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** weatherboards, balustrade, brackets and lattice painted warm white; front door and stair handrails painted deep heritage green; broken lattice panels replaced; gutter straightened.
**Keep:** rusty roof, stair structure.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** new light grey corrugated metal roof, same shape; new timber stairs in the same position with the same number of steps, painted white with green handrails.
**Keep:** everything else.
