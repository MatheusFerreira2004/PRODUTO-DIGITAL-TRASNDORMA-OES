## The recipe

**The story:** a timber house on stumps with a deep verandah, left to the weather. Classic white and green brings back the character.

**Palette 20 · Queenslander White & Green**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#F5F2EA` | `#F5F2EA` | `#3E5E4E` | `#3E5E4E` | Light grey metal |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear overgrowth from the yard and under the house | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Sweep and wash the verandah gently | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Ferns and pots on the verandah; simple front bed | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint weatherboards, lattice and balustrade | 2 | Check first (lead, asbestos), then DIY at ground level | High |
| Paint the front stairs | 2 | DIY if the stairs are sound | Medium |
| Restumping | 3 | Pro (structural) | — |
| New corrugated metal roof | 3 | Pro | — |
| Rebuild front stairs | 3 | Pro | — |

**Order:** clear → check stumps, stairs and materials → structural and roof work (pro) → paint → verandah styling and garden.

**Watch out:** many older Queenslanders have fibro (possibly asbestos cement) in enclosed verandahs or underneath. Older timber may have lead paint. Test before sanding. Heritage overlays can control colours: check with your council.

**Most common mistake:** painting a verandah whose stairs or stumps are moving. Fix the structure first.

## Production

**Before:** traditional Queenslander house, single storey raised on timber stumps about 6 ft (1.8 m) high, wide front verandah across the full front with white timber balustrade and decorative brackets, corrugated metal hip roof, central timber stairs to the verandah, timber lattice screening under the house, weatherboard walls. Condition: abandoned; paint peeling to grey timber, rusty roof, tall weeds and palms overgrown, broken lattice panels, sagging gutter.

**Level 1 — change:** weeds and overgrowth cleared from the yard and under the house; verandah swept clean; a few potted ferns on the verandah; simple garden bed either side of the stairs with tropical shrubs; lawn mowed.
**Keep:** peeling paint, rusty roof, broken lattice, gutter.

**Level 2 — change:** weatherboards, balustrade, brackets and lattice painted warm white; front door and stair handrails painted deep heritage green; broken lattice panels replaced; gutter straightened.
**Keep:** rusty roof, stair structure.

**Level 3 — change:** new light grey corrugated metal roof, same shape; new timber stairs in the same position with the same number of steps, painted white with green handrails.
**Keep:** everything else.
