## The recipe

**The story:** years of overgrowth hide a solid little bungalow. Most of the change comes from clearing, paint and a simple garden.

**Palette 01 · Classic Cream**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Shutters | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#E8E2D0` | `#F5F3EE` | `#6E7F8A` | `#2B2F33` | Charcoal |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear weeds, vines and overgrowth | 1 | DIY | High |
| Mow, edge, mulched beds with low shrubs | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Pots by the door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint siding, trim, columns and shutters | 2 | DIY, check first (lead, rot) | High |
| Replace broken railing pickets | 2 | DIY if only pickets; Pro if the railing is loose | Low |
| New mailbox | 2 | DIY | Low |
| New roof | 3 | Pro | — |
| New concrete steps and path | 3 | Pro | — |
| Porch light | 3 | Pro (electrician) | — |

**Order:** clear the yard → check wood and test old paint → roof (if doing it) → paint → railing and details → garden last.

**Watch out:** a house like this is likely to be older than 1978, so test paint before scraping or sanding, including the door. Climbing vines can be poison ivy: cover skin, wash tools and clothes afterwards, and never burn the cuttings.

**Most common mistake:** painting before clearing the vines. Roots and stems left in the siding push the new paint off.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Clear-up kit, plus a string trimmer for the long grass
- Door kit: blue-grey exterior door paint
- Bed kit: mulch for two beds along the front, 6-8 small low shrubs
- Pot kit

### Level 1 plan: two weekends
On a house this overgrown, the clear-up alone fills a weekend.

| | Saturday | Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| Weekend 1 | Cut vines at the base; pull them off walls and columns once dry. Trim long grass. | Rake, bag the waste, clear the path and steps. Mow. |
| Weekend 2 | Door: clean, sand (after a lead test), prime. Dig and edge the two beds. | Door: two coats. Plant shrubs, mulch, set out the pots. |
