## The recipe

**The story:** years of overgrowth hide a solid little bungalow. Most of the change comes from clearing, paint and a simple garden.

**Palette 01 · Classic Cream**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Shutters | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#E8E2D0` | `#F5F3EE` | `#6E7F8A` | `#2B2F33` | Charcoal |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clear weeds, vines and overgrowth | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Mow, edge, mulched beds with low shrubs | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Pots by the door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint siding, trim, columns and shutters | 2 | DIY, check first (lead, rot) | High |
| Replace broken railing pickets | 2 | DIY if only pickets; Pro if the railing is loose | Low |
| New mailbox | 2 | DIY | Low |
| New roof | 3 | Pro | — |
| New concrete steps and path | 3 | Pro | — |
| Porch light | 3 | Pro (electrician) | — |

**Order:** clear the yard → check wood and test old paint → roof (if doing it) → paint → railing and details → garden last.

**Watch out:** a house like this is likely to be older than 1978, so test paint before scraping. Climbing vines can be poison ivy.

**Most common mistake:** painting before clearing the vines. Roots and stems left in the siding push the new paint off.
