## The recipe

**The story:** a newer box-shaped house in builder's beige with bare dirt in front. A dark palette and warm timber accents make it look designed.

**Palette 13 · Charcoal & Cedar**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#3C4043` | `#3C4043` | `#A0673E` | `#A0673E` | Black |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low-maintenance garden: grasses, gravel, one feature tree | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door warm timber colour or stain a timber door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Large modern planters at the entry | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint walls charcoal (ground floor) | 2 | DIY on single storey; Pro above | High |
| Timber slat screen beside the entry | 2 | DIY for a freestanding screen; Pro if fixed to the house structure | Medium |
| New cladding section | 3 | Pro | — |
| Wall lights | 3 | Pro (electrician) | — |

**Order:** paint → slat screen → garden last.

**Watch out:** dark colours absorb heat. Check that the paint and the wall material are suitable for dark colours, especially vinyl and some composite claddings.

**Most common mistake:** too many accents. One warm timber element is enough against charcoal.

## Production

**Before:** recent single-storey simple modern house, flat-fronted with a low skillion roof, beige render walls, black window frames, single garage with a beige door, plain front door, small concrete porch. Condition: plain; bare dirt and a few weeds in front, no planting, builder's beige everywhere.

**Level 1 — change:** front yard laid out with grey gravel, ornamental grasses and one small multi-stem feature tree; front door painted warm cedar colour; two tall black cylindrical planters at the entry.
**Keep:** wall colour, garage door, roof.

**Level 2 — change:** walls and garage door painted charcoal; a freestanding vertical cedar slat screen beside the entry.
**Keep:** windows, roof, garden.

**Level 3 — change:** a vertical cedar cladding panel around the entry section; two slim black wall lights beside the door, switched off.
**Keep:** everything else.
