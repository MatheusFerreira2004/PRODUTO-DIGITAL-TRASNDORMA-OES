## The recipe

**The story:** a newer box-shaped house in builder's beige with bare dirt in front. A dark palette and warm timber accents make it look designed.

**Palette 13 · Charcoal & Cedar**
| Walls | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#3C4043` | `#3C4043` | `#A0673E` | `#A0673E` | Black |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planting bed along the house: gravel and grasses | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door a warm timber colour or stain a timber door | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Two tall modern planters at the entry | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Finish the front yard: gravel, grasses, one feature tree | 2 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint walls charcoal (ground floor) | 2 | DIY on single storey; Pro above | High |
| Timber slat screen beside the entry | 2 | DIY for a freestanding screen; Pro if fixed to the house structure | Medium |
| New cladding section | 3 | Pro | — |
| Wall lights | 3 | Pro (electrician) | — |

**Order:** paint → slat screen → garden last.

**Watch out:** dark colours absorb heat. Check that the paint and the wall material are suitable for dark colours, especially vinyl and some composite claddings.

**Most common mistake:** too many accents. One warm timber element is enough against charcoal.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Rake, spade, weed mat, edging strip
- Grey gravel for a bed about 3 ft (90 cm) deep along the front of the house, 2 in (5 cm) deep
- Ornamental grasses suited to your area, planted in a repeating row
- Door kit: warm cedar-colour exterior door paint, or exterior timber stain for a timber door
- Pot kit: two tall black cylindrical planters

### Level 1 plan: one weekend
| | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Weed and rake the whole yard level. Mark the bed along the house. | Door: clean, sand, prime or prepare for stain. Lay edging and weed mat. |
| Sunday | Door: first coat. Plant the grasses, spread the gravel. | Door: second coat. Planters at the entry. |

## Production

**Before:** recent single-storey simple modern house, flat-fronted with a low skillion roof, beige render walls, black window frames, single garage with a beige door, plain front door, small concrete porch. Condition: plain; bare dirt and a few weeds in front, no planting, builder's beige everywhere.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** a neat bed along the front of the house with grey gravel and a row of ornamental grasses; the rest of the yard weeded and raked level, still bare soil; front door painted warm cedar colour; two tall black cylindrical planters at the entry.
**Keep:** wall colour, garage door, roof, bare soil in the rest of the yard.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** whole front yard finished in grey gravel with ornamental grasses and one small multi-stem feature tree; walls and garage door painted charcoal; a freestanding vertical cedar slat screen beside the entry.
**Keep:** windows, roof, planters, door colour.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** a vertical cedar cladding panel around the entry section; two slim black wall lights beside the door, switched off.
**Keep:** everything else.
