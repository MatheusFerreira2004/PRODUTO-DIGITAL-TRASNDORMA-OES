## The recipe

**The story:** a rendered house with stains, hairline cracks and a grey, tired look. Warm earth colours and a dry garden suit it.

**Palette 17 · Terracotta Render**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#D9B99B` | `#F4EDE3` | `#5B6B4E` | `#5B6B4E` | Terracotta tile |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gentle wash of the walls | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Gravel strip along the house with a few drought-tolerant plants | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Two large terracotta pots; paint the front door olive | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Extend the gravel garden across the front yard | 2 | DIY | Medium |
| Fill hairline cracks and paint walls with masonry paint | 2 | DIY on ground floor; check first if cracks are wider than hairline | High |
| Paint window trim and gate | 2 | DIY | Medium |
| Re-render damaged areas | 3 | Pro | — |
| New roof tiles | 3 | Pro | — |

**Order:** wash → look at the cracks → repairs → paint → garden.

**Watch out:** wide cracks, stepped cracks or bulging render can mean moisture or movement. Check first before covering them.

**Most common mistake:** using ordinary wall paint. Render needs a masonry paint suitable for exterior use.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Garden hose, soft-bristle brush, bucket, mild cleaner suitable for render
- Spade, rake, weed mat, edging strip
- Gravel for a strip about 2-3 ft (60-90 cm) wide along the front wall, 2 in (5 cm) deep
- Drought-tolerant plants suited to your area
- Pot kit: two large terracotta pots
- Door kit: olive green exterior door paint

### Level 1 plan: one weekend
| | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Wash the walls gently, bottom up. Clear the dead lawn along the front wall. | Door: clean, sand, prime. Lay edging and weed mat. |
| Sunday | Door: first coat. Plant, then spread the gravel. | Door: second coat. Pots by the door, rake the rest of the yard. |

## Production

**Before:** single-storey rendered house with a low terracotta tile roof, smooth grey-beige render walls, arched porch opening at the front door, two square windows, low rendered front wall with a metal gate. Condition: worn; dark water stains under the windows, hairline cracks, flaking paint near the base, dry dead lawn, rusty gate.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** walls cleaned (cracks still visible, paint still faded); a neat gravel strip along the front wall with a few drought-tolerant shrubs; two large terracotta pots by the door; front door painted olive green; the rest of the dry lawn raked but still dry.
**Keep:** wall colour, cracks, roof, gate, most of the dry lawn.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** gravel garden extended across the whole front yard with ornamental grasses and drought-tolerant shrubs; cracks filled and walls painted warm terracotta-sand; window frames and the low front wall cap painted warm off-white; gate painted olive.
**Keep:** roof, arch, pots, door colour.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** render smooth and even everywhere; roof re-tiled in fresh terracotta tiles, same shape.
**Keep:** everything else.
