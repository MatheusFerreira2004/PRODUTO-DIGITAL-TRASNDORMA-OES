## The recipe

**The story:** tan brick, cream trim, aluminium windows. Instead of painting the brick, dark trim makes it look current.

**Palette 23 · Tan Brick Update**
| Brick | Trim | Door | Accent | Roof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tan (existing) | `#3A3D42` | `#C27C3E` | `#3A3D42` | Dark grey |

| Change | Level | Who | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gentle wash of the brick | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Replace overgrown shrubs with grasses and succulents | 1 | DIY | Medium |
| Paint the front door warm timber-orange | 1 | DIY | Low |
| New letterbox | 1 | DIY | Low |
| Paint fascia, gutters and garage door charcoal | 2 | DIY at single-storey height, with care | Medium |
| Paint window frames charcoal | 2 | Check first (suitable paint for aluminium) | Medium |
| Roof restoration or new roof | 3 | Pro | — |
| New driveway | 3 | Pro | — |

**Order:** wash → paint fascia, gutters, frames → garage door → garden.

**Watch out:** houses from this era in Australia may have asbestos cement eaves linings. Do not sand, drill or pressure-wash the eaves until tested. High-pressure washing can also damage old mortar.

**Most common mistake:** painting the brick white straight away. Dark trim often gives a modern look without the maintenance.

### Level 1 shopping list
- Garden hose, soft-bristle brush, bucket, mild masonry cleaner
- Clear-up kit (to remove the old shrubs)
- Bed kit with gravel mulch instead of bark; ornamental grasses and succulents suited to your area; weed mat
- Door kit: warm timber-orange exterior door paint
- Letterbox and fixings suited to brick or a post

### Level 1 plan: one weekend
| | Morning | Afternoon |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | Wash the brick by hand, working from the bottom up. Remove old shrubs. | Door: clean, sand, prime. Lay weed mat. |
| Sunday | Door: first coat. Plant grasses and succulents, add gravel. | Door: second coat. Fix the letterbox, edge the lawn. |

## Production

**Before:** 1970s Australian single-storey brick house, tan-cream brick, low-pitched concrete tile roof in terracotta, wide eaves with cream lining, cream aluminium sliding windows, cream metal gutters and fascia, single garage with a cream roller door on the right, small porch with a slim brick column. Condition: dated; dry patchy lawn, overgrown shrubs, stained brick at the base, faded orange tiles.

**Level 1 (front) — change:** brick cleaned; lawn tidied with a crisp edge; overgrown shrubs replaced with a bed of ornamental grasses and succulents with gravel mulch; front door painted warm timber orange; new simple black letterbox.
**Keep:** cream trim, windows, garage door, roof.

**Level 2 (front) — change:** fascia, gutters, downpipes, window frames and garage roller door painted charcoal.
**Keep:** brick, roof, door colour.

**Level 3 (front, then aerial) — change:** roof restored to a dark charcoal tile, same shape; new exposed-aggregate concrete driveway in the same position.
**Keep:** everything else.
