# Bonus 3 · Facade checklist

Print it. Tick only what is done. Mark N/A what doesn't apply.

## Before you start
- [ ] Photo of the house taken (front and angle)
- [ ] AI "after" images made and checked
- [ ] Every change labelled DIY / Check first / Pro
- [ ] Local rules checked (HOA, council, heritage, strata)
- [ ] Old paint tested (lead) / suspect sheets tested (asbestos) where relevant
- [ ] Quotes requested for Pro jobs

## Level 1 · Weekend
- [ ] Weeds, vines and clutter cleared
- [ ] Lawn mowed and edged
- [ ] Garden beds shaped and mulched
- [ ] Path, steps and porch swept
- [ ] Front door cleaned, sanded, primed and painted
- [ ] Pots and doormat at the entry

## Level 2 · One month
- [ ] Walls gently washed and dry
- [ ] Loose paint removed safely; bare spots primed
- [ ] Trim painted
- [ ] Ground-floor walls painted
- [ ] Shutters painted, added or removed
- [ ] Broken pickets, boards or railings replaced
- [ ] Hardware, mailbox and numbers in one finish

## Level 3 · Full (professionals)
- [ ] Roof
- [ ] Concrete or paved path, steps, driveway
- [ ] Porch or verandah structure
- [ ] Outdoor lighting (electrician)
- [ ] Upper-floor painting

## Finish
- [ ] Garden planted last
- [ ] Leftover paint labelled (colour, date, area)
- [ ] Waste disposed of correctly
- [ ] Before and after photos taken from the same spot
