# 10 quick wins

The ten smallest changes that make the biggest difference from the street. Most take an hour or two. None needs a professional.

[IMAGE: quickwins_grid · 10 small before/after crops of the same house: door, hardware, edges, pots, mat, windows, mulch, path, clutter, light glass]

| # | Quick win | Time | Kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Clear the clutter.** Bins out of sight, hoses coiled, dead plants and broken pots gone. | 1 h | Clear-up kit |
| 2 | **Paint the front door.** The single change people notice first. | Weekend (drying time) | Door kit |
| 3 | **Match the hardware.** Handle, knocker, mailbox and house numbers in one finish: black, bronze or brass. | 1-2 h | Screwdriver, new hardware |
| 4 | **Edge the lawn.** A crisp line between lawn and bed or path makes everything look cared for. | 1-2 h | Half-moon edger or spade |
| 5 | **Fresh mulch.** One even, dark layer about 2-3 in (5-7 cm) deep. | 2 h | Bed kit |
| 6 | **Weed the path and steps.** Pull weeds from cracks and sweep. | 1 h | Weeding tool, stiff broom |
| 7 | **A matching pair of pots.** One each side of the door, same pot, same plant. | 1 h | Pot kit |
| 8 | **A new doormat,** wide enough for the door or wider. | 10 min | Doormat |
| 9 | **Clean windows and frames** on the front of the house, ground floor only. | 1-2 h | Bucket, mild cleaner, squeegee, soft brush |
| 10 | **Clean the porch light glass** and replace a dead bulb with the same type. Changing the fitting itself is a job for an electrician. | 15 min | Cloth, bulb |

**Keep it gentle:** do not use a high-pressure washer on old paint, old render or sheets that could contain asbestos. See *From image to reality*.

## The kits

The example houses refer to these kits so their shopping lists stay short. Use generic products; quantities are typical starting points.

### Clear-up kit
- Heavy gloves, long sleeves, eye protection
- Loppers and a pruning saw
- Rake, garden fork, stiff broom
- Garden waste bags or a green-waste bin
- String trimmer (buy, borrow or hire) for long grass

### Door kit
- Mild cleaner or degreaser and a sponge
- Sanding sponges, about 120 and 220 grit
- Exterior primer suited to the door material
- Exterior door paint (satin or gloss); about 1 qt / 1 L usually does one door, two coats
- 2 in (50 mm) angled brush and a small foam roller
- Low-tack painter's tape, drop cloth, screwdriver

**Older homes:** if the house was built before 1978 (US) or before 1990 (Canada), or the age is unknown, test the door paint for lead before sanding. In Australia, homes built before 1970 are the main concern. See *From image to reality*.

**Order:** remove hardware → clean → sand lightly → prime → two thin coats, waiting the recoat time on the label → refit hardware once dry.

### Bed kit
- Spade, half-moon edger, rake
- Mulch: a 2 cu ft bag covers about 8 sq ft at 3 in deep; a 50 L bag covers about 0.65 m² at 7.5 cm
- Plants: ask a local nursery what grows well in your climate and in that much sun

### Pot kit
- Two matching pots, at least 16 in (40 cm) wide for an entry
- Potting mix, a layer of gravel or a pot foot for drainage
- Two matching plants
